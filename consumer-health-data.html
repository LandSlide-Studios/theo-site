Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy — Theo (BibleLock)

Effective Date: August 31, 2026 Last Updated: August 31, 2026 Version: 1.0

This is a separate, standalone policy, required by the Washington My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373) and Nevada SB 370. It exists on its own because those laws require a distinct consumer health data policy with its own link, not a section buried inside a general privacy policy.

It applies to residents of Washington and Nevada, and to anyone else who wants to know how Theo handles data that touches wellbeing. Theo is offered in the United States and 143 other territories; it is not offered in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland. Our general Privacy Policy governs everything else, and where the two overlap, this one controls for consumer health data.

1. What We Treat as Consumer Health Data

Washington defines "consumer health data" broadly — any information linkable to a consumer that identifies their past, present, or future physical or mental health status. Theo is a Bible study and focus app, not a health app, and we collect no diagnoses, medications, lab results, biometrics, genetic data, or reproductive health information.

We nonetheless treat the following as consumer health data, because a broad reading of the statute could reach them and we would rather over-protect than argue about it later:

Data Why it may qualify
Fasting session records Start and end times of voluntary fasts you log — arguably bodily function or nutrition data
Focus session history Patterns of attention and device use — arguably behavioural health data
Spiritual needs and obstacles Free-text selections such as anxiety, loneliness, or discouragement
Conversations with Theo touching wellbeing Messages you choose to send about emotional or mental state
Journal entries touching wellbeing Reflections you write that mention how you are doing

We do not collect: precise location, biometric identifiers, health-provider records, prescription data, reproductive or sexual health data, gender-affirming care data, or any information from a health care provider.

2. How We Collect It

Only from you, and only because you entered it. We do not buy it, we do not infer it from third-party sources, and we do not obtain it from data brokers, advertising networks, or your other apps.

3. Why We Use It

  • To run the features you switched on — the fasting tracker, the focus timer, your journal
  • To let Theo respond to what you actually said, when you choose to talk to it
  • To show you your own history and progress

That is the entire list. We do not use consumer health data for advertising, marketing, profiling, scoring, research, or product analytics keyed to health signals.

4. Who We Share It With

We do not sell consumer health data. We have never sold it. Selling it would require your separate written authorisation under RCW 19.373.100, and we have never sought one and do not intend to.

We share it only with processors who run the service on our behalf, under contract, and only for the purposes above:

Processor What it receives Why
Supabase Journals, fasting and focus records, spiritual profile Stores your account so it survives a new device
Anthropic Message text and journal excerpts you opted in, when you use the AI assistant Generates Theo's reply
OpenAI Text of journals you opted into "Theo can read" Generates the embedding that makes search work
Amazon Web Services Summarised conversation memory Gives Theo continuity between sessions

Fasting records and focus history are never sent to any AI provider. Neither AI provider trains models on your data.

We will disclose consumer health data if legally compelled by valid process, and we will tell you unless we are barred from doing so.

5. Your Consent

We ask before we collect, and separately before we share:

  • Fasting and focus tracking are opt-in features. Not using them means no data.
  • Spiritual needs and obstacles are optional onboarding questions. You can skip them.
  • The AI assistant requires you to tap Agree & Continue on a screen that names Anthropic and OpenAI and describes what is sent, before your first message.
  • "Theo can read" is off by default on every journal entry. Nothing is sent to OpenAI until you turn it on for that specific entry.

Withdrawing consent is available at any time in Settings → Privacy. Withdrawal stops future collection and sharing. It does not undo what already happened, which is what deletion is for.

6. Your Rights

Washington and Nevada residents have the right to:

  • Know whether we collect, share, or sell your consumer health data, and who has received it
  • Access it, including a list of every third party it has been shared with
  • Withdraw consent to collection and to sharing
  • Delete it — including from our records and from every processor listed in Section 4

How to exercise them:

  • In the app: Settings → Delete My Data or Settings → Delete Account
  • By email: tommy@landslidestudios.com, subject line "Consumer Health Data Request"

We respond within 45 days, extendable once by 45 days where reasonably necessary, and we will tell you if we extend and why. Verification is proportionate — writing from your registered email is normally enough. There is no charge.

On deletion, we delete from our active systems and notify every processor in Section 4 to do the same, as RCW 19.373.080 requires. Backups roll off within 30 days.

If we refuse, you may appeal by replying to our decision. We will respond in writing within 45 days. If we still refuse, you may complain to the Washington State Attorney General at atg.wa.gov/file-complaint or the Nevada Attorney General at ag.nv.gov.

Private right of action. Washington's Act is enforceable through the state Consumer Protection Act, which means a violation may give you a private claim. We would rather you told us first, but that right is yours regardless.

7. Geofencing

We do not use geofencing. Theo does not request, collect, or process precise location data at all. We operate no geofence around any health care facility, and we could not build one — the location permission is not in the app.

RCW 19.373.110 prohibits geofencing health care facilities to collect health data or send health-related advertising. We comply by not touching location in the first place.

8. How Long We Keep It

Data Retention
Fasting and focus records (on device) Until you delete them or remove the app
Fasting and focus records (account) Life of the account; deleted within 30 days of a deletion request
Journals and spiritual profile Life of the account; deleted within 30 days
Chat message text Not retained — discarded once the reply is delivered
Conversation memories 180 days, then automatically expired
Copies held by AI providers Up to 30 days

9. Changes

Material changes will be notified in the app before they take effect. Where a change concerns data we process on your consent, we will ask again rather than assume.
